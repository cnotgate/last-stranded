This is a note made by Charlie Wickes detailing the events following the explosion on Observation Satellite Horizon. If anyone is willing to read this, please remember our story and the betrayal of those we follow.

After the explosion of Horizon, we on Sanctuary decided to tally our loses. The damage that we suffered were the loss of the majority of our rations, 6 injured, and 5 unluckily died. One of the injured, Rex was significantly wounded and suffered immense blood loss that leads to his death. His pained screams still echoed in my mind and the way he called for his mom betrays his usual demeanor. But considering what happened after, he and the others that died may be extremely lucky. 

After assessing the damage, we managed to perform first aid on the injured and gathered whatever resources we could get. Emma, our comms expert, managed to receive a transmission from Horizon before it exploded. The information that we received were shocking enough that we didn't say anything for five minutes while we just stood there watching at the screen. There was a clear image on the screen, with the rays of the Sun lighting the backdrop of the image, we can see Earth, or what's left of it, being devoured by some sort of black mass that warps light around it like an eclipse. The image was as beautiful as it is horrifying and it tells us one thing clearly, the Earth somehow got devoured by a black hole. Even me, one of those who grew on Mars really felt the sense of immense loss at what just had happened.

After that, we poured over the data five times over trying to find some mistake in the data that was gathered, but with every attempt, we only became more sure that there was no mistake. With nothing else to do, we counted again what's left of our rations and are met with more bad news, it won't last through the day for us all. One of us, Kain suggested that we take deep sleep, but the amount of deep sleep liquid that's available is only enough for four people.

We decided to try to send a distress signal to Mars, but the explosion seems to have blown the Mars relay off course and dealt some minor damage that needs to be fixed. Luckily Captain Adler arrived at Sanctuary with the mini-shuttle and he said he's willing to go there to fix the Mars relay. But, before he departs we told him the result of the analysis and then he just stood there for while. I've worked with him for many years, and yet it was the first time i ever saw him like that. After a while, he managed to make an obvious fake smile and finally head out.

And then those of us in Sanctuary started to talk. Just a simple talk, but somehow, it devolved into an intense argument. After a while, we noticed that the operation's leader, Bradley along with his two subordinates left the scene. Then, an ominous sound of a thruster igniting resounded from the shuttle hangar. The last shuttle that we have, the only way to go back to Mars, slowly drifts away with from our sight. After rechecking our resources, we found out that they took three packs of deep sleep liquids.

And now we arrive at this point. I have suffered true hunger before, so i know what will happen to this station. Slowly, the hunger will make us desperate and on some point, someone will not differentiate between human flesh and animal flesh and it will soon became a slaughterhouse. Before that, i volunteered to take my own life because i don't want to go through that moment again. Three other people followed my decision, as for the rest, some are indecisive and others have a terrifying glint on their eyes. The good thing for those who sacrifice themselves is that we have a painless way to go out by utilizing the emergency cryo pods. It'll be cold, but because we don't use the deep sleep liquid, we'll just die painlessly.

One last message...
For those who read this.
DO NOT GO INTO SANCTUARY.
